# Argument 

An argument is a sequence of [[statement]] aimed at demonstrating the truth of an
**assertion**. the assertion at the end of the sequence is called the
**conclusion** and the preceding statements are called **premises**

**example** :

- Premise 1: All men are mortal
- Premise 2: Socrates is a man
- Conclusion: Therefore, Socrates is mortal
