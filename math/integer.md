# Integer

An **integer** is any whole number: positive, negative, or zero. The [[set]] of
all integers is denoted $\mathbb{Z}$

$$
\mathbb{Z} = \{\ldots, -3, -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, 3, \ldots\}
$$

## Subsets of integers

Integers can be classified by various properties

- **[[even number]]** — divisible by 2
- **[[odd number]]** — not divisible by 2
- **[[prime number]]** — greater than 1, only divisible by 1 and itself
- **[[composite number]]** — greater than 1, has a divisor other than 1 and itself

Every integer is also a [[rational number]] (take $b = 1$ in $\frac{a}{b}$)
