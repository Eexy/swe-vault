# Implicit quantification

Let $P(x)$ and $Q(x)$ be [[predicate|predicates]] and suppose the common
[[predicate#domain|domain]] of $x$ is $D$

- The notations $P(x) \implies Q(x)$ means that every element in the [[predicate#truth-set|truth set]] of $P(x)$ is in the truth set of $Q(x)$ or equivalently $\forall x, P(x) \rightarrow Q(x)$
- The notations $P(x) \Leftrightarrow Q(x)$ means that $P(x)$ and $Q(x)$ have identical truth sets or equivalently $\forall x, P(x) \leftrightarrow Q(x)$
