# N choose R

Let $n$ and $r$ [[integer]] with $0 \le r \le n$ The symbol

$$
\binom{n}{r}
$$

is read **n choose r** and represents the numbers of [[subset]] of size $r$ that
can be chosen from a [[set]] with $n$ elements

Because it is a number of subset it will always be an [[integer]]

Furthermore for all integer $n$ and $r$ with $0 \le r \le n$ we have

$$
\binom{n}{r} = \frac{n!}{r!(n-r)!}
$$
