# Properties of summations and products

If $a_m, a_{m+1}, a_{m+2}, \ldots$ and $b_m, b_{m+1}, b_{m+2}, \ldots$ are sequences of real numbers and $c$ is any real number, then the following equations hold for any integer $n \geq m$:

1. $\displaystyle\sum_{k=m}^{n} a_k + \sum_{k=m}^{n} b_k = \sum_{k=m}^{n} (a_k + b_k)$

2. $\displaystyle c \cdot \sum_{k=m}^{n} a_k = \sum_{k=m}^{n} c \cdot a_k$ — generalized distributive law

3. $\displaystyle\left(\prod_{k=m}^{n} a_k\right) \cdot \left(\prod_{k=m}^{n} b_k\right) = \prod_{k=m}^{n} (a_k \cdot b_k)$
