Let $b$ be a positive real number with $b \neq 1$. For each positive [[set#Common set|real number]] $x$, the **logarithm with base b of x**, written $log_{b}x$ is the exponent to which $b$ must be raised to obtain $x$. Symbolically

$$
log_{b}x = y \leftrightarrow b^y = x
$$

The **logarithmic function with base b** is the [[function]] from $R^+$ to $R$ that takes each positive real number $x$ to $log_{b}x$

## Properties of logarithms

For any positive real numbers $b$, $c$, $x$ and $y$ with $b \neq 1$ and $c \neq 1$ and for every real number a:

- $log_b(xy) = log_b(x)+ log_b(y)$
- $log_b(\frac{x}{y}) =  log_b(x) - log_b(y)$
- $log_b(x^a) = a \cdot log_b(x)$
- $log_c(x) = \frac{log_b(x)}{log_b(c)}$
