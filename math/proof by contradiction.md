# Proof by contradiction

The method of **proof by contradiction** is based on the fact that either a
[[statement]] is true or it is false but not both

So if we can show that the assumption that a fiven statement is not true leads
logically to a **contradiction**, impossibility or absurdity

Here is the step to follow : 

1. Suppose the statement to be proved is dalse. That is, suppose that the
   negation of the statement is true
2. Show that this supposition leads logically to a contradiction
3. Conclude that the statement to be proved is true


**example** : There is no greatest [[integer]]

**Proof.** Suppose that is a greatest integer $N$

Then for $N \geq n$ for every integer $n$

Let $M = N+1$. Now $M$ is an integer sinci it is the sum of integers. Also $M > N$
since $M = N + 1$

This $M$ is an integer that is greater than $N$. So $N$ is the greatest integer
and $N$ is not the greatest integer, which is a contradiction

This is a contradiction. Hence the supposition is false and the theorem is true
